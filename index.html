How it works

Collect. Bank. Share.

One system of record for the physical specimen — from the family's kitchen table to the researcher's bench.

Collect

Kit-by-mail, on schedule

Kits generated straight from the visit and event schedule, tracked family to lab — because your registry is global, not down the hall.

Bank

Receiving, storage, custody

Intake, condition checks, and freezer location logged on arrival — with a full chain of custody on every biospecimen and derivative.

Share

Request, approve, distribute

A researcher requests, your access committee approves against consent, and the shipment goes out — with results reported back to the community.
